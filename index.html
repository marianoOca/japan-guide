Four ways guys fumble Tokyo

    I met people who really regretted where they stayed. From the right block, the bars are a five-minute walk and so is your bed.

  • Hunting as a pack of guys

    A group of guys at the club can feel kind of creepy. With one or two girls in the group you're cool, you're normal. There is a place to find them first.

  • Golden Gai with no yen

    Most of the bars are cash only, and almost every one charges a cover just to get in. Pull yen before you go.

    A love hotel sells a two-hour rest and an overnight stay. I once got trapped in a rest: walk out of the room and they charge you in full.
